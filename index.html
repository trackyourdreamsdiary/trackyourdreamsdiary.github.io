Track Your Dreams

A dream journal for Android · Un diario de sueños para Android

Track Your Dreams helps you remember and understand your dreams. Write them down or record them by voice as soon as you wake up, see your patterns in daily, calendar and stats views, and, if you want, share selected dreams with friends on the community Board.

  • Write or dictate your dreams in seconds.
  • Audio recordings stay on your phone only.
  • Private entries are visible only to you.
  • Optional Google account link to recover your journal on a new phone.

Track Your Dreams te ayuda a recordar y entender tus sueños. Escríbelos o grábalos con tu voz apenas despiertes, mira tus patrones en las vistas diaria, calendario y estadísticas y, si quieres, comparte algunos sueños con tus amigos en el Board de la comunidad.
